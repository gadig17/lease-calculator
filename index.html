Negotiation Quick Tips

Negotiate the selling price first, just like buying. Never start with monthly payment.
Ask the dealer for the "buy rate" money factor. They can mark it up — check Edmunds forums for the base rate.
Residual value is not negotiable — it's set by the bank/manufacturer. Higher residual = lower payment.
Ask if the acquisition fee can be waived or reduced, especially on luxury brands.
Putting $0 down is usually smarter — if the car is totaled, you lose your down payment. GAP insurance covers the lease balance.
Check manufacturer websites for current incentives and rebates before going to the dealer.
The 1% rule: a monthly payment at or below 1% of MSRP (pre-tax, $0 down) is a good deal. Below 0.8% is excellent.
